A simple three-step playbook

Buy in early, make the company list-ready, then cash out — repeatedly, with no fee standing between shareholders and the return.

01

Buy in early

Take equity stakes in profitable companies, typically 3–5 years before they list, at a low price.

02

Make it list-ready

In-house audit, advisory and merchant-banking teams prepare the company for a clean public listing.

03

Cash out

Exit via IPO or secondary sale, then redeploy into the next opportunity in the pipeline.
